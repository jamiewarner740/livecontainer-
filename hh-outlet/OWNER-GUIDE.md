# H&H Outlet: how to run the store

Sign in at **/admin** on your phone. The bar at the bottom has the five things you do most.

## List an item (List item)

1. **Photos.** Tap **Take photos**. Get the front, the label or barcode, and any damage up close. The first photo is the main one.
2. **Barcode (optional).** Tap **Scan** (Android) or type the numbers (iPhone).
   - If you already have that item, the app says so. Open the existing listing and add stock instead.
   - Otherwise the name is filled in for you. Check it: the same barcode can be a different color, size or bundle.
3. **Name, category, condition.** One tap each. **Tested?** helps buyers trust you.
4. **Price.** Retail is optional. Only enter one you can back up, such as the price printed on the box.
5. **Publish.** Or **Save draft** to finish later.

**Remembered between items:** category, shelf and pallet. Items from the same pallet go faster.

**More details** has these optional fields:
- description
- what's included, missing parts and known defects
- shelf or bin
- pallet
- your cost
- weight (needed for eBay)
- "pickup only" for big items

## Sell at the counter (Sell)

1. **Find the item.** Type the SKU on its label (like HH-1042), scan its barcode, or type part of the name.
2. **Check the price.** Change it if you agreed a different one; that change is noted.
3. **Choose how they paid:** cash, card or other. Take cards on your Square reader as usual.
4. **Tap Record sale.** Stock goes down, the website stops selling it, and eBay is updated.

## Orders (Orders)

**To fulfill** shows what needs doing.
- **Pickup orders:** tap **Mark ready for pickup**, then **Mark picked up** when the buyer collects.
- **Shipping orders:** enter the carrier and tracking number, then tap **Mark shipped**. For eBay orders, eBay is told too.
- **Each item's shelf is listed,** so you can find it.

**Refunds** (owner only): **Cancel and refund** returns the money through Square and puts the items back in stock. Refund eBay orders in eBay instead.

## Stock

Search by name, SKU, barcode or shelf. Tick several items to act on them all at once:
- publish them
- move them to drafts
- change the price by a percentage (for example −20 for clearance)
- set the category or shelf
- list them on eBay, or take them off

**Filters:**
- **Older than 30/60/90 days:** stock that isn't moving.
- **Low stock / Sold out.**

Open an item to:
- edit it
- add photos
- change stock (with a reason)
- see its full history

## Pallets

Add each pallet you buy: what you paid, freight and other costs. Pick it when listing items from that pallet. Its page then shows what it has earned on each channel, and whether it has paid for itself yet.

## Today, Insights, Activity

- **Today:** sales by channel, stock value, and anything needing attention.
- **Insights:**
  - what shoppers searched for and didn't find (good for choosing pallets)
  - items nobody is seeing
  - items people view but don't buy
- **Activity:** every change, who made it and when.

## eBay

1. **Connect.** Tap **Connect eBay** and sign in on eBay's own page. The site never sees your eBay password.
2. **Settings.** Choose your shipping, payment and return policies (made in eBay Seller Hub), your ship-from ZIP, and any markup to cover eBay fees.
3. **List.** Tick **eBay** when listing, or use **List on eBay** in Stock.

**Your old eBay listings:** paste their item numbers under **Bring in listings** and they arrive as drafts.

**Sold in both places at once:** the eBay page shows it under **Sold on both channels at once**. Contact the eBay buyer, then mark it handled.
